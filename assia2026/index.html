Welcome

ASSIA returns on December 13, 2026, in Singapore as the Asian-Pacific School on Search and Information Access.

We welcome students, early-career researchers, and researchers from academia and industry to learn about information retrieval and information access, share their ideas, and connect with lecturers and fellow participants.

Further details about the school will be added to this website as they become available.
