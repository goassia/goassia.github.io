Registration

ASSIA 2026 will be held on December 13, 2026, as part of the SIGIR-AP 2026 tutorial program. Participants must register for SIGIR-AP 2026 and purchase at least a Workshop only registration to attend ASSIA.

  • Full Passport: An option for participants who also wish to attend the main conference.
  • Workshop only: The minimum registration required to attend ASSIA 2026.

Please visit the SIGIR-AP 2026 registration page for fees, registration instructions, and the full details of what each registration type includes.
